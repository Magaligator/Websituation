How much does a 3D website cost in Switzerland?

The price of a 3D website depends on the scene, the models and the number of pages. Here are the cost factors, our displayed plans and what is quoted individually.

What makes the price vary

There is no single price for a 3D website, because two “3D” projects can be very different. The main factors:

  • The complexity of the scene: a simple object to rotate, or a configurator with many options;
  • The state of your 3D models: already available and suited to the web, or to be created and lightened;
  • The number of pages and content: a full site with text, FAQ, blog, language versions;
  • The surrounding features: online quote, shop, customer area, form;
  • Performance requirements: scene weight, mobile version, fallback image, accessibility.

Our plans and the 3D quote

At Websituation, prices are displayed. On the Pricing page:

  • Presque Free: CHF 399 per year, plus CHF 150 one-off production fee;
  • PMEPro: CHF 2,350 in the first year, then CHF 1,350 from the second;
  • FlexiPro: on quote, for custom needs.

A custom 3D project falls under FlexiPro: it is quoted individually, after a free first meeting. We do not announce a generic range, as it would be misleading: the quote takes your scene, your models and your pages into account.

What is included

Our plans include the domain, hosting, launch and follow-up. For a 3D project, the precise needs (model weight, expected traffic, follow-up) are defined in the quote. The schedule is calculated at the start of the project, once the scene, pages and content are defined, and confirmed with your dedicated manager.

How to control the budget without sacrificing quality

  • Start with a single, well-chosen scene, then enrich it later;
  • Reuse your existing models rather than creating new ones;
  • Limit the options to the most useful ones for your customers;
  • Plan the project in stages: a 3D page first, a configurator afterwards;
  • Take care of the text: it is what brings visitors from Google, even before the scene.
