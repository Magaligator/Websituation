The client

A. Murith SA is a family-run funeral home founded in 1888 on boulevard de la Cluse, opposite Geneva's cantonal hospital. It supports families with funerals, formalities, national and international transport, and offers funeral pre-planning contracts.

The goal

Guide families clearly through a difficult moment: quickly find who to call, understand the steps and get a quote, day or night.

What we did

  • A multi-page site organised around families' needs: funerals and bereavement, chapels, coffins, flowers, formalities, international transport, pre-planning;
  • An emergency block and a quote-request button always in view;
  • A bilingual French and English site, with a blog on costs, pre-planning and steps after a death;
  • Local SEO work around "funeral services Geneva" and Google Ads campaigns, with a results report;
  • Matching printed materials: brochures, leaflets and pens.

What the site brings the client

Families immediately find the 24/7 emergency line, understand the services and can request a quote. The tone stays calm and restrained, like the house itself.
