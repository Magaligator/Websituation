A 3D website puts an object, a place or a story in three dimensions right inside the page: visitors rotate it, explore it, configure it. Done well, it loads fast, stays readable on a phone and ranks like any other site. We design 3D websites from Cologny (Geneva) for businesses across Switzerland.

What is a 3D website?

It is a site where part or all of the experience relies on three-dimensional elements shown in the browser, with no software to install: a product you view from every angle, a building you walk through, an animated journey that follows the page scroll. The rest of the site (text, menus, contact form) is a classic website, essential to be understood by visitors and by Google.

3D is useful when it shows something photos do not show well:

  • a product to look at closely, customise or compare;
  • real estate and venues: tour, plan, volumes;
  • a portfolio or a brand that wants to stand out;
  • an event or a launch with strong staging;
  • industry: a machine, a part or a process to explain.

If you mainly need to present your business simply, a classic website will be a better fit, and we will tell you so.

What 3D brings

Used with restraint, 3D serves three goals: holding attention longer than a still image, explaining a product or a place without long text, and setting you apart from competitors whose sites look alike. It is not an end in itself. We promise no conversion figure: the effect depends on your offer, your visitors and the quality of the content. What can be measured, we follow with you after launch.

The types of 3D sites we build

  • Immersive showcase: a home page with a 3D object or scene presenting the company, on a complete site (services, contact, blog);
  • Product configurator: visitors pick colours, materials or options, see the result, then request a quote;
  • 360° tour: a place, showroom or building site explorable from the page;
  • Scroll story: the page tells a story by animating 3D elements as you scroll.

To sell online, 3D combines with an online shop; for a more restrained presentation, with a responsive showcase website.

We choose the lightest technology for each need: 3D through browser transformations for a simple object, like the cube on our home page, up to more advanced solutions when the project requires it. To understand the options, read our guide to creating a 3D website.

3D and Google ranking

A 3D scene is an animated image: Google does not read its content. A 3D site therefore ranks thanks to everything around the scene, which is where most 3D sites fail. Our rules:

  • text stays real text in the page (headings, paragraphs, FAQ), never drawn inside the 3D scene;
  • 3D loads after the first display: the page is readable at once, the scene follows;
  • a fallback image is shown while loading, or if the browser cannot handle 3D, with a description for screen readers;
  • models are lightened (compressed web formats) to keep the page fast on mobile;
  • animations respect the “reduce motion” setting of visitors who ask for it.

How we work

  1. Discussion: a free call to understand what you want to show, to whom, and whether 3D is the right answer;
  2. 3D mock-up: the object or scene, the page journey and the text, validated together before development;
  3. Integration: the scene is built into a complete site, adapted to phones;

FAQ

How much does a 3D website cost in Switzerland?

It depends on the complexity of the scene, the state of your 3D models, the number of pages and the features. A 3D project is priced by quote (FlexiPro plan), after a free first call. The prices of our other plans are shown on the Pricing page.

How long does it take to build a 3D site?

The schedule is set with your account manager at the first call, depending on the scene, the models available and the number of pages.

Is a 3D site bad for Google ranking?

No, provided the text stays in the page, the 3D loads after the first display and the models are lightened. How the site is built is what matters, not the presence of 3D.

Does a 3D site work on a phone?

Yes. We design mobile-first, with lightened models and a fallback image if the device or connection cannot display the scene.

Who provides the 3D models?

You can provide your existing files (product, plans, models), which we adapt for the web. If you have none, creating the models is specified in the quote.

Are hosting and maintenance included?

Domain and hosting are included in our subscriptions. For a 3D project, the exact needs (model weight, traffic) and follow-up are defined in the quote.

Is a 3D site accessible to everyone?

We provide a text description of the scene, navigation that works without a mouse and respect for the “reduce motion” setting.

Do you work outside Geneva?

Yes. We meet clients in Geneva and French-speaking Switzerland and work remotely for the whole of Switzerland.
