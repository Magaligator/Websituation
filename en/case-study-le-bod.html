The client

Le Bod is a fitness studio in Geneva's old town, on rue Chausse-Coq. It presents itself as Switzerland's first Lagree Micro Pro studio and offers hybrid classes combining Lagree, Mat Pilates, cardio and restorative yoga to a demanding, design-conscious clientele.

The goal

Give the website the same feel as the studio: elegant, bright, close to an art gallery. And make booking the simplest step of the journey, on a phone as on a computer.

What we did

  • Designed the site structure around booking: home, buy classes, schedule, member area;
  • Put the welcome offer (buy two classes, get one free) up front on the home page;
  • Staged press, client reviews and the team to reassure visitors;
  • Presented the three signature classes: BODCAMP, SCULPT THAT BOD and RESTORATIVE BURN;
  • Matching stationery: business cards in the studio's identity.

What the site brings the client

Visitors understand the offer, choose a class, buy a package and book without leaving the site. The studio's image stays consistent from the first photo to the business card.
