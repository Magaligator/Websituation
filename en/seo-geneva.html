SEO in Geneva

Be found when your customers search: audit, keywords, technical work, content and local visibility in Geneva and French-speaking Switzerland.

A beautiful site is useless if nobody finds it. Search engine optimisation (SEO) makes your site clear to search engines and useful to your visitors, so you show up when customers look for what you offer. For a local business, it is often the most profitable channel.

Why SEO matters

Before calling a business, most people type a few words into Google. Searches such as "web agency Geneva" or "funeral services Geneva" express an immediate need. If you don't appear, your competitors take your place. Our article on why Google ranking is essential for a small business covers this in detail.

Our five-part approach

  1. Starting audit: state of the site, existing pages, competitors, keywords your customers use;
  2. Keywords and structure: one page per service, with a unique main heading and description;
  3. Technical work: speed, mobile adaptation, HTTPS security, optimised images, structured data;
  4. Content: service pages, FAQs, articles that answer real questions;
  5. Local presence and reputation: Google Business Profile, consistent contact details, customer reviews.

Local SEO in Geneva

For a Geneva business, the Google Business Profile and consistent name, address and phone number across all your channels matter as much as the website itself. We help you organise this information and ask for reviews in a simple, regular way.

What is included in our plans

  • Presque Free: listing on Google and basic organic SEO;
  • PMEPro: Google Analytics statistics to follow your visits;
  • FlexiPro: SEO and Google Ads (SEA), with Google Analytics and Search Console.

How long until you see results?

Indexing a new site can take days or weeks. Earning good rankings generally takes several months of regular work. SEO is an investment: its effects build up and last, unlike advertising, which stops when the budget stops.

What we don't promise

We don't guarantee first place on Google: nobody can honestly do that. We commit to what depends on us: a technically healthy site, useful content and transparent follow-up. For immediate visibility, Google Ads campaigns complement SEO well.

Examples

For the A. Murith SA funeral home, the site was structured around families' searches and supported by Google Ads campaigns. The Le Bod site is organised around the classes customers want to book.

Key takeaways
  • SEO makes your site clear for Google and useful for your visitors.
  • It works in five parts: audit, keywords, technical, content, local.
  • Results take several months; no position is guaranteed.
