A showcase website presents your business, your services and your contact details: it is the business card your customers check before they call or drop by. We build responsive showcase websites in Geneva and French-speaking Switzerland, meaning sites that adapt automatically to the size of the screen.

What is a showcase website?

It is a simple, focused site with no online shop: it says who you are, what you offer and how to reach you. It suits freelancers, shopkeepers, practices and small businesses that want a professional presence on Google without piling up pages.

Why responsive matters

Many of your customers discover you from their phone. A responsive site rearranges its content to stay readable on a small screen: text you can read without zooming, buttons that are easy to tap, images that fit. All our sites are designed mobile-first, then adapted to tablets and computers. Google also takes the mobile display into account when ranking your pages (see our approach to SEO).

The five pages of a showcase site

  • Home: your main message and a clear call to action;
  • Services: what you offer, explained simply;
  • About: who you are and why to trust you;
  • Work or references: concrete examples of what you do;
  • Contact: phone, email, address and a form.

Each page covers one precise topic with a clear heading, so it can rank for what your future customers search.

FAQ

What is a responsive website?

A site that automatically adapts its layout to the size of the screen: phone, tablet or computer. Visitors can read and browse comfortably on any device.

How many pages does a showcase site have?

Our starter plans include five pages: home, services, about, work and contact. More pages can be added with a custom plan.

Are the domain and hosting included?

Yes, they are included in the plans, along with the launch. A professional email address is included in PMEPro and FlexiPro.

Can I edit my showcase site afterwards?

Yes. Your manager takes care of it: the PMEPro plan includes 12 changes a year with support within 48 hours. Training is also available if you want to do it yourself.
