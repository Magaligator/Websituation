The client

Mybodytree is a shea butter supplier from northern Ghana. The brand works across the whole chain (cooperatives, collectors, processors, distributors, NGOs) and supports women producers to improve living conditions in rural areas.

The goal

Tell a story and a concrete impact, build trust with partners and buyers, and make it easy to get in touch.

What we did

  • A brand story in sections: our story, how we work, our communities;
  • Key figures up front: 2 cooperatives, over 150 women processors, 2,000 women collectors, 30 communities;
  • Photos and a video of the cooperative to show the field;
  • A clear English menu: Home, About, Products, Sustainability, Contact us, Jobs;
  • A "Let's work together" contact form, plus domain and hosting.

What the site brings the client

A professional online presence that makes the impact visible and credible, in a natural green palette, accessible from Ghana as from Switzerland.
