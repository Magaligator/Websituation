Your website is the first thing customers look at before they call, visit or buy. We build fast, mobile-friendly websites designed to be found on Google, in Geneva and French-speaking Switzerland, from a five-page showcase site to an online shop.

A site that gets found and convinces

A good site answers three questions within seconds: who are you, what do you offer, how can I contact you? We start from your business and your customers to build the site structure, organise the content and choose the calls to action. Each page covers one precise topic with a clear heading, so it can rank for what your future customers search (see our approach to SEO).

What is included

  • A site on a customised template or fully custom, mobile-friendly;
  • Domain, hosting and, depending on the plan, a professional e-mail address;
  • Content integration and optimisation, listing on Google and basic SEO;
  • Google Analytics statistics (PMEPro plan);
  • E-commerce, chatbot, CRM and automation for more ambitious projects (FlexiPro plan);
  • Training if you want to learn to manage your own site.

Template or custom site?

Two approaches for two needs. A customised template is the fastest and most affordable way to get online; a custom build gives a unique result that reflects your brand.

  • Presque Free: CHF 399 + CHF 150 one-off production fee, 5-page site on a customised template;
  • PMEPro: CHF 2,350 in the first year, then CHF 1,350 from year 2, fully custom site up to 5 pages, 12 changes a year, campaigns and materials included;
  • FlexiPro: on quote, with e-commerce, SEO and Google Ads, CRM, chatbots and automation.

See the details and comparison on the Pricing page.

How we work

  1. Discover: a free call to understand your business, your customers and your goals;
  2. Design: visual identity, mock-ups and content approved together before anything goes live;
  3. Launch: domain, hosting, SEO: your site is published and visible on Google;
  4. Grow: statistics, updates and campaigns with your dedicated manager.

How much does a website cost in Geneva?

The price depends on the number of pages, features (booking, shop, member area), the content to produce and the follow-up you want. Rather than make you wait for a quote, we display our subscription prices: you know from the start what you invest, and what is included (domain, hosting, launch, follow-up).

Sites you can visit

Four finished sites are live: Le Bod, a fitness studio in Geneva; A. Murith SA, a funeral home; Murith Prévoyance, funeral pre-planning; and Mybodytree, a shea brand from Ghana. Each has a case study, and you can see them all on the Websites page.

FAQ

How long does it take to build a website?

With a ready-made template (Presque Free), launch is quick. For a fully custom site, the schedule is set with your manager during the first call, depending on pages and content.

Can I edit my site myself?

Yes if you wish: we offer training to manage your site. Otherwise your manager takes care of it; the PMEPro plan includes 12 changes a year with support within 48 hours.

Will my site work on phones?

Yes. All our sites are designed mobile-first, then adapted to tablets and computers.

Are domain and hosting included?

Yes, they are included in the plans, along with launch. A professional e-mail address is included in PMEPro and FlexiPro.

Do you build online shops?

Yes, in the FlexiPro plan, with pages and features defined on quote (e-commerce, CRM, chatbots, automation).
