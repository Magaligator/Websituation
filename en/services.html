Frequently asked questions.

What is included in a plan?

Domain, hosting, launch and basic SEO are included. PMEPro adds a professional e-mail, Google Analytics statistics, 12 changes a year, two digital campaigns and two communication materials.

How long to get online?

With a ready-made template (Presque Free), launch is quick. For a fully custom site, the schedule is set with your manager during the first call.

Who handles changes to my site?

Your dedicated manager. PMEPro includes 12 changes a year with support within 48 h.

Can I move from one plan to another?

Yes: you can move up to a higher plan or to tailor-made (FlexiPro) as your business grows.
