Print and branding in Geneva

Business cards, brochures, leaflets, flyers and items: one consistent identity from web to print, with print-ready files.

Your image doesn't stop at the screen. A business card, a leaflet or a pen lands in your customers' hands: a chance to extend your website's identity into the real world. We design printed materials consistent with your site, from the idea to the print-ready file.

Visual identity as a starting point

Logo, colours, typography and tone form the foundation of all your materials. We define them (or build on yours) so that your site, your cards and your brochures are recognised at a glance.

The materials we design

  • Business cards, including gold foil or special finishes;
  • Flyers, two- and three-panel leaflets and brochures;
  • Stationery: gift cards, cards, letterheads;
  • Promotional items: pens, bottles and other goodies;
  • Social media visuals matching your campaigns.

From web to print: examples

For Murith Prévoyance, the brochure reuses the site's codes: same red, same messages. For the Murith house, leaflets, a booklet and pens extend a calm, reassuring image. Other projects, such as gold-foil stationery for a brand or a set of custom pens, are on the Work page.

From idea to print

  1. Brief: use, quantity, format, budget;
  2. Design: mock-ups for your approval;
  3. Preparation: print-ready files (formats, bleed, colours);
  4. Delivery: production follow-up with the printer of your choice or ours.

Included in our plans

The PMEPro plan includes two communication materials of your choice (for example business cards, leaflets, brochures or promotional items), on top of the website and two digital campaigns. For wider needs, get in touch.

Key takeaways
  • Your visual identity should be recognisable on the site as on paper.
  • Cards, leaflets, brochures, stationery, items: print-ready files.
  • PMEPro includes two communication materials of your choice.

FAQ

Do you provide files for the printer?

Yes: we prepare print-ready files with the right formats, bleed and colour profiles.

Can you use my existing logo?

Yes. We start from your identity if you have one, or build it with you if it needs defining or refreshing.

Who handles the printing?

We coordinate production with the printer of your choice or the one we work with, depending on the material and budget.

How many materials are included in PMEPro?

Two communication materials of your choice, on top of the website and two digital campaigns.
