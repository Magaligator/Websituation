About Websituation

The story of a cube put back in order one face at a time: that of a Geneva agency that likes untangling complicated situations.

It all starts with a situation. A website that looks dated. Visibility that has stalled. Brochures that no longer look like the business. A budget to respect, little time, and the feeling that digital is a puzzle reserved for specialists. Websituation was born from that observation: behind every digital project, there is first a situation to understand.

One cube, one logic

Our emblem is a cube turned in every direction. Scrambled, it looks impossible to put back in order; approached with method, it can be solved. An agency's work is much the same: you can't get one face right by undoing the others. A beautiful website without visibility stays invisible, a campaign without a landing page wastes its budget, brochures unrelated to the website blur the message.

That is why we bring together website design, SEO, print and branding and digital campaigns in one place, and why every project starts by looking at the whole before touching a single piece.

Five colours, five convictions

  • Yellow, creativity: ideas that set you apart and visuals that leave a mark;
  • Orange, flexibility and transparency: plans that evolve with you and prices announced from the start;
  • Green, client satisfaction: a dedicated manager, support within 48 hours, results that matter;
  • Blue, confidentiality: your data and your customers' data handled with care;
  • Red, innovation: chatbots, artificial intelligence and automation, used with judgement.

A small team with great attention

We chose to stay human-sized. A deliberately small client portfolio means time to listen, to understand a trade and to suggest what really fits, not what is easiest to sell. Every client has a dedicated manager who knows their story and goals.

Stories that never look alike

A fitness studio that wants to feel like an art gallery. A funeral home founded in 1888 that must reassure at any hour. A shea brand telling the impact of women's cooperatives in Ghana. Three situations, three voices, one demand for clarity. Find them on the Websites page.

What's next?

Digital moves fast, and so do we: artificial intelligence, chatbots, new formats. Our rule doesn't change: test, explain, and keep people at the centre. The next chapters are written with new projects, and perhaps yours.

Let's write the next chapter together

It all starts with a free call to understand your situation and goals. Book a call: we get back to you within 48 business hours, in French or English. You can also explore our plans and their prices.

Key takeaways
  • An independent agency in Cologny that looks at the whole before touching a piece.
  • Five convictions: creativity, flexibility and transparency, client satisfaction, confidentiality, innovation.
  • A deliberately small client portfolio and a dedicated manager for each.

How do we work with you?

On subscription, with published prices, a free initial call and a dedicated manager throughout the project.
